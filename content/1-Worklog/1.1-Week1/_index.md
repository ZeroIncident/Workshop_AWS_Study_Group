### Week 1 Objectives:

* Build a practical understanding of cloud computing, including how AWS delivers services on demand and why organizations use cloud platforms.
* Become familiar with the AWS learning environment, the AWS Management Console, and the structure of the Cloud Journey program.
* Complete foundational exercises covering account setup, cost awareness, identity management, networking, compute, storage, and databases.

### Week 1 Achievements:

* Completed introductory exercises for AWS accounts, cost management, IAM, VPC, EC2, S3, RDS, Cloud9, and Lightsail.
* Gained confidence navigating AWS service pages and reviewing the main configuration choices for common cloud resources.
* Established a foundation for later hands-on work by connecting identity, networking, compute, storage, and database concepts.
