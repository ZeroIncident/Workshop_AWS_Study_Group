### Week 10 Objectives:

* Learn multiple AWS deployment approaches for web applications, including Elastic Beanstalk, Amazon EC2, ECS, and Fargate.
* Explore Kubernetes concepts with Amazon EKS and Red Hat OpenShift Service on AWS.
* Understand the purpose of data lakes and the basic steps needed to organize and ingest data on AWS.

* Reviewed deployment workflows for Node.js, WordPress, Elastic Beanstalk, EC2, ECS, and AWS Fargate.
