### Week 7 Objectives:

* Learn how CI/CD pipelines automate application delivery from source changes through deployment.
* Explore AWS storage, workflow orchestration, cost-analysis, and data-query services used in operational environments.
* Understand the planning considerations involved in moving a monolithic application toward a microservices architecture.

### Week 7 Achievements:

* Reviewed CodePipeline stages, triggers, and automated deployments to understand a repeatable release workflow.
* Practiced evaluating Storage Gateway, FSx, DynamoDB, Step Functions, performance metrics, and cost-optimization reports.
* Used Glue and Athena concepts for cost-data analysis and created a high-level migration plan for a microservices architecture.
