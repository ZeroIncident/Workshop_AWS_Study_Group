### Week 8 Objectives:

* Explore how microservices and event-driven patterns improve the flexibility and scalability of modern applications.
* Learn the core parts of a serverless application, including APIs, Lambda functions, storage, databases, authentication, and deployment automation.
* Practice connecting application components through APIs, events, custom domains, and messaging services.

* Planned microservices boundaries and event flows while reviewing how application data and workflows can be restructured.
