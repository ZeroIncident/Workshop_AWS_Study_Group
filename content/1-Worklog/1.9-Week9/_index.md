### Week 9 Objectives:

* Continue developing serverless application skills with a focus on deployment, monitoring, GraphQL, CRUD operations, and API integration.
* Learn the architecture of a serverless document-management system, including storage, authentication, delivery, search, and operational tooling.
* Understand how distributed tracing and monitoring help diagnose issues across a serverless application.
